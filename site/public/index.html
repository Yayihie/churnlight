Who to save this week.

Churnlight reads the exports you already have and tells you which customers are slipping — with the reason in plain words and what to do next. Free and open source.

Get it free on GitHub Pro — $49/mo

How it works

01

Drag in your CSVs. Stripe export, support tickets, product usage, meetings. Whatever you have — missing files are fine.

02

Six transparent rules score every account. Champion left. Renewal coming with no contact. Usage down. Stale tickets. Failed payments. Cancelled meetings.

03

Monday morning, you get the list. Each account: why it's at risk, what to do, and the MRR on the line. Attach it to email or paste it into Slack.

Why not Gainsight?

Because you're a team of two, not a team of twenty. Enterprise CS platforms want a dedicated admin, a migration project, and a five-figure invoice — then your CSMs do data entry twice. Churnlight has no admin, no dashboards to worship, and no black-box health score that says a customer is "green" the week they cancel. Every signal is a rule you can read and a slider you can move.
