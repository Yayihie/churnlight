How it works

Three steps. Then it's just a list you act on.

01

Drag in your exports

Stripe, support tickets, product usage, meetings — whatever you have. Missing files are fine; it works with what you drop.

02

Seven rules score every account

Champion left. Renewal coming with no contact. Usage down. Stale tickets. Failed payments. Cancelled meetings. Seats going cold. Each one weighted, each one visible.

03

Monday morning, the list arrives

Every account: why it's at risk, what to do, and the MRR on the line. Attach the email or paste it straight into Slack.

The rules

Every signal is a rule you can read.

Health scores that say "green" the week a customer cancels aren't health scores. Churnlight shows you exactly what fired and why — then lets you move the thresholds with sliders, not code.

What's included

Everything. Free. Everyone.

There is no paid tier. Every feature below ships in the repo, MIT licensed, for anyone.

Core engine
Risk scannerScores every account against 7 weighted rules — champion gone, renewal with no contact, failed payments, usage drop, cancelled meetings, stale tickets, seats going cold
Why / Do cardsEvery flagged account ships with the reason in plain words and the specific next move
Rule slidersEvery threshold and weight is adjustable in the UI — no code, no black box
MRR at stakeRisk list is ordered by dollars on the line, not alphabetically
Input
Drag-and-drop CSVsUsage events, tickets, meetings, payments, user seats — drop whatever exports you have; missing files are fine
One-click pulls
Stripe auto-connectPaste a Stripe secret key and payments pull in directly — no CSV exports
HubSpot & SalesforcePaste a private app token (or Salesforce credentials) and accounts, deals, tickets and cases normalize straight into the scanner — no exports, no mapping
Output & delivery
Web appZero-dependency server on localhost:8788 — your data never leaves your machine
Email digestsFormatted HTML + Markdown + Slack-ready output, one click each
Share linksOne click makes a read-only link to this week's risk list — no login for your boss, expires in 7 days
Session restoreYour files and rule settings are remembered locally — next Monday, drop nothing, just hit the button
Monday schedulerJoin the Monday list with your email — the risk list is ready every Monday at 8:00 AM automatically
